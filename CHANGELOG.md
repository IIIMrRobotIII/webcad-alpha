# Changelog / Sürüm Notları

All notable updates and releases for **WebCAD Alpha** are documented here.

---

## [v0.1.0-alpha] — 2026-11-24

### Initial Public Launch / İlk Canlı Yayın
- **Live Web Application:** Launched [webcad-alpha.vercel.app](https://webcad-alpha.vercel.app/) (TR) and [webcad-alpha.vercel.app/en/](https://webcad-alpha.vercel.app/en/) (EN).
- **2D & 3D Parametric CAD Engine:** Sketching, Extrude, Revolve, Hole, Fillet, Chamfer, Shell, Boolean CSG, Section Plane, and Transform Gizmo.
- **Export & Import:** Export models in **STL**, **STEP**, **OBJ**, and **CAD/JSON** formats; import existing models/projects.
- **Bilingual Support (TR ↔ EN):** Live language switching in Settings with instant UI, status prompt, and 3D ViewCube label updates.
- **Live Material Switching:** Apply `Steel`, `Aluminum`, `Brass`, `Polymer`, or `Gold` materials to solids in real time.
- **Accounts & Supporter Automation:** Google and verified email sign-in, 3 free downloads/day, and automatic unlimited access for Buy Me a Coffee supporters.
